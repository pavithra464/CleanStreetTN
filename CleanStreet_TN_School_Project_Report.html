Executive Summary (What is CleanStreet TN?)

CleanStreet TN is an independent, community-driven civic web application designed to help citizens and students map open garbage piles, promote segregation, and quickly route complaints directly to verified municipal helplines (such as Greater Chennai Corporation 1913 and Tamil Nadu CM Helpline 1100).

1. Report

Snap a photo of unattended trash, pinpoint the street on an interactive map, and submit.

2. Track

Get a unique tracking reference (CSTN-YYYY-XXXXX) to follow real-time progress.

3. Route

Directly connect with official helplines (1913, 1100, Swachhata App) with pre-filled details.

Problem Statement & Motivation

The Problem We Observed:

  • Open dumpsites and overflowing waste bins cause breeding grounds for mosquitoes (dengue, malaria) and foul odors.
  • During the northeast monsoon in Tamil Nadu, road waste clogs stormwater drains, leading to severe street waterlogging.
  • Citizens often want to complain, but they don't know their ward number, official contact, or helpline.

Why I built this: As an 8th-grade student, I wanted to prove that technology and coding can be used not just for video games, but to solve real problems right outside our school and homes. CleanStreet TN turns every resident with a smartphone into an active neighborhood caretaker.

Key Features of the Website

A

Garbage Reporting Form with Photo & GPS

Users capture photos of garbage and select categories (overflowing bin, dead animal, plastic waste). The app automatically compresses images in the browser (<5MB) so it works quickly even on slow mobile data connections.

B

Interactive Cleanliness Map (GIS)

Built with open-source Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. Displays color-coded pins across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu. Citizens can filter by status (Submitted, In Progress, Resolved).

C

Smart Municipal Grievance Router

Automatically identifies if the location is in Chennai (routes to Greater Chennai Corporation 1913 / Namma Chennai App) or other districts (routes to CM Helpline 1100 and Collectorate portals).

D

Bilingual Interface (English & தமிழ்)

With a single click on the top navigation bar, the entire website converts into Tamil (க்ளீன்ஸ்ட்ரீட் தமிழ்நாடு), ensuring elderly citizens and Tamil-medium students can use it easily.

E

Educational Hub & Waste Quiz

Contains 8 environmental lessons on source segregation (wet vs dry vs sanitary), plastic bans, and composting, plus an interactive quiz with instant scores for students.

F

School & Community Eco-Club Drives

Enables school eco-clubs and neighborhood associations to post clean-up drives, recruit student volunteers, and track the kilograms of waste collected.

Technology Stack Explained

Layer Technology Why We Chose It
User Interface React 18 + TypeScript Loads instantly without reloading the page, with strict type safety.
Styling & Design Tailwind CSS Responsive layout that looks great on mobile phones, iPads, and PCs.
Maps & GIS Leaflet + OpenStreetMap 100% free and open-source mapping without expensive commercial API charges.
Data Analytics Recharts Interactive pie charts and bar graphs for municipal waste trends.
Database Supabase & LocalStorage PostgreSQL backend with offline demo mode so the app works anywhere.

Faculty Q&A (Answers to Expected Teacher Questions)

Q1: "Is this connected to the official government server?"

Answer: "No, Ma'am/Sir. CleanStreet TN is an independent civic portal. It acts as an easy-to-use bridge for citizens and empowers them with verified direct links and helplines (GCC 1913 and TN 1100), rather than acting as a replacement for the government."

Q2: "What if someone uploads a prank photo or false complaint?"

Answer: "We built an Admin Moderation Page (at /admin). Every report can be reviewed by a teacher or moderator before it is officially verified on the public map."

Q3: "Did you pay for Google Maps?"

Answer: "No, we used Leaflet and OpenStreetMap, which are open-source and free. This means our school or city can run this project with zero map licensing costs."

Q4: "What is your future plan for this project?"

Answer: "In the future, I want to add AI-based image classification so that when a student snaps a photo, the app can automatically detect whether it is plastic, organic, or construction debris."
